# Mini Project Proposal

## Title
Mapping Connections Between Canadian Government Contractors and Policymakers

## Overview
This project will examine connections between major Canadian government contractors, corporate leadership, lobbying activity, and former public officials. The goal is to map how companies receiving public contracts may be connected to policymakers through board memberships, executive roles, lobbying records, and professional biographies.

This project does not aim to prove corruption or wrongdoing. Instead, it will identify patterns of institutional overlap and “revolving door” relationships between the public and private sectors.

## Research Question
How are Canadian government contractors connected to policymakers, former public officials, and lobbying networks?

## Data Needed
I plan to collect data on:

- company names
- contract values
- number of contracts
- government departments awarding contracts
- board members and executives
- former government roles of individuals
- lobbying registrations
- target departments in lobbying records
- source links for verification

## Possible Comparisons
I want to compare:

- firms with former public officials in leadership vs. firms without them
- contract value vs. number of political/government connections
- lobbying activity vs. contract awards
- which people or companies appear most central in the network

## Expected Output
The final output will include a small cleaned dataset, a network visualization, and a short analysis of key patterns connecting companies, individuals, and government departments.
