# Why the Big Four?

This project focuses on the Big Four consulting firms in Canada:

- Deloitte
- PwC
- EY
- KPMG

These companies work closely with governments through consulting, auditing, digital transformation projects, and public-sector advisory work. They also often hire former government officials, policy advisors, and senior public servants.

Because of their strong connections to government institutions and federal contracting, the Big Four are a useful case study for exploring relationships between public officials and private consulting firms.

By focusing on a small group of influential companies, this project can build a manageable network dataset linking contracts, company leadership, lobbying activity, and former government officials.
